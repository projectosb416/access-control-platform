PART 2 - RECOVERY PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AUDIENCE
  Whoever opens this envelope in an emergency. No technical background
  assumed. Read this entire page before doing anything.

WHAT THE PEPPER IS
  A 64-character string written on the card above. It protects every PIN
  on the platform. It is used by the software to check whether a visitor's
  PIN is correct.

WHEN TO USE THIS
  Open this envelope ONLY IF:
    - The primary operator (name on the envelope) has been unreachable for
      more than 7 days AND the platform is failing to process PINs
    - The platform is being transferred to a new operator
    - You are instructed by the primary operator to do so

  Do NOT open this envelope if:
    - The primary operator is simply away for a few days
    - The platform is working normally
    - You are curious what is inside

HOW TO RECOVER
  1. Fill in the Backup Contact name and phone number on the next section
     of this document, if not already filled in.
  2. Contact the Backup Contact. Explain that you have opened this envelope
     and why.
  3. The Backup Contact will either instruct you on what to do next, or
     will take over from here.

WHAT TO DO WITH THE PEPPER
  Do not send the pepper value by SMS, email, WhatsApp, or any messaging
  app. Do not read it aloud over a phone call. Do not photograph it.

  If you must transmit it, use a method the Backup Contact specifies
  directly. Otherwise, hand the physical card to them in person.

PART 4 - WHAT NOT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do not:
  - Share the pepper with anyone except the Backup Contact
  - Post it online, anywhere, in any form
  - Type it into any app, website, or service without instruction
  - Discard this envelope even after use
  - Assume it is unimportant because it looks like a random string

If this envelope is lost, stolen, or suspected of being opened by someone
unauthorized, tell the primary operator or Backup Contact immediately.
The pepper will need to be rotated (replaced with a new one), which is a
significant operation.
